Starbucks Sales \& Business Performance Analytics



An end-to-end sales analytics project built using Microsoft Excel, Power Query, Power Pivot, and DAX.



This project focuses on analyzing sales performance, customer behaviour, product profitability, and store performance through interactive dashboards and business insights.



Project Overview



The objective of this project was to turn raw sales data into meaningful business information and understand how different factors affect overall sales performance.



I worked on the complete analytics workflow, starting with data cleaning and transformation, followed by data modelling, DAX calculations, KPI development, and dashboard creation.



Note: This is an independent practice project using a Starbucks-style synthetic dataset. It is not an official Starbucks project and is not affiliated with Starbucks Corporation.



Tools \& Technologies



\- Microsoft Excel

\- Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation

\- Power Pivot – Data modelling and relationships

\- DAX – Calculated columns, measures, and business KPIs

\- Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts – Data analysis and visualization



Project Workflow



1.Data Preparation: Cleaned and transformed raw datasets using Power Query.

2.Data Modelling: Created relationships between customers, orders, products, stores, Region and calendar data using Power Pivot.

3.DAX Calculations: Developed measures to calculate sales, profit, margins, order metrics, and growth indicators.

4.Dashboard Development: Built interactive dashboards with KPI cards, charts, and slicers.

5.Business Analysis: Identified performance trends and translated findings into business insights.

Key Business Findings



\- North was the highest-performing region, while East recorded the lowest sales.

\- Merchandise and Food were the leading sales categories compared with Beverages.

\- UPI was the leading payment method by sales.

\- Annual sales declined from approximately ₹73.56 lakh in 2024 to ₹70.73 lakh in 2025.

\- Q4 was the strongest quarter in 2024, while Q2 was the strongest quarter in 2025.

\- Monthly sales showed fluctuations rather than a consistent upward trend.



2026 data covers January to October, so it should not be directly compared with full-year figures.\*
